Behind the scenes of my science fair project

How muchFORCE?to knock down a toy

Here is how I did my experiment!

Why I picked force

I picked force because I like it. In the Angry Birds game, you have to launch the birds and try to break the box.

Force is a push or a pull. A launcher gives a ball a big push so it can fly and knock things over.

My big question

I wanted to find out how much force is needed to knock down a toy.

What I used

  • Rubber bands
  • Cloth (for the pouch)
  • A foam platform
  • 2 wooden dowels
  • Hot glue and cardstock
  • 3 foam balls (small, medium and big)
  • 4 foam obstacles
  • 4 small toy pups

Building the launcher

My dad helped me by building the launching thing to launch and hit the puppies.

It is a box with two wooden posts, rubber bands and a cloth pouch. You put a ball in the pouch, pull it back and let go. The colors on top show how far you pull back.

Pink = medium pull Purple = large pull

What I did

  1. Set up the foam obstacles and the toy pups so the pups are grouped in the center.
  2. Choose a ball.
  3. Put the ball in the launcher, pull it back to a marked distance and aim for the pups.
  4. Count how many pups fell over.
  5. Do it again with another ball size. Keep the obstacles the same!

What happened

What I learned

I learned about how much force you need to launch a little ball, a big ball and a medium-sized ball to knock down the puppies and dodge the obstacles.

My chart shows:

  • The little ball only knocked down 1 pup, even with a big pull.
  • The medium ball knocked down all 4 pups with a large pull.
  • The big ball knocked down all 4 pups with just a medium pull.

More pull and a bigger ball = more force = more pups down!

Then we made a game!

And he also made a whole game about it.

In Angry Pups, you pull back a launcher just like my real one. The Pull meter even uses low, medium and large, just like my experiment. The pups and foam balls in the game look like my real toys!

What I want to tell people

It's fun to play with force, and force is fun. Force is, like, everywhere!

Thanks for checking out my project!
